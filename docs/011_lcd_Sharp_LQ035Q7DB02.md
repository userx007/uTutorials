A few notes that match what you photographed:

- **LD0–LD17** is an 18-bit RGB parallel data bus straight from the i.MX27's LCD controller (LCDC)
- **TOP/BOTTOM/LEFT/RIGHT** (pins 31–34) are the four analog resistive-touch electrodes — this lines up with the touch overlay you can see laminated onto the glass
- **VR1**, the trimmer pot you saw near the connector, is explicitly called out in the manual as the **flicker/contrast adjustment** — turn it to stabilize the display if it flickers, ideally with a plastic (non-conductive) tool since it's typically adjusted with power applied
- **3V15** (pin 1) is the 3.15V rail (this matches the "P15V" test point you saw — likely a labeling variation, or it could be a separate LCD bias supply generated on the daughter card itself by that MAX1747 DC/DC chip)
- **CPLD_LCDON** (pin 20) is the enable signal — this is generated by the ADS board's CPLD, so the LCD board can't power up standalone without that base board driving it
